About Winxlibrary

Empowering data-driven decisions across desktop, mobile, and web platforms.

Winxlibrary is a software development initiative focused on creating practical, reliable, and high-quality digital tools for data analysis, nutrition programs, and field survey workflows.

Our mission is to simplify complex data processes for researchers, analysts, and program teams—especially in public health, nutrition, and development sectors. We build solutions that support data collection, validation, analysis, and reporting across different environments.

Winxlibrary develops cross-platform tools to meet real-world needs:

  • Desktop Applications: Tools like IYCF Analyse for advanced survey data validation and reporting.
  • Mobile Applications (APK): Field-friendly apps such as NutDiag, designed for on-device data collection and screening.
  • Web-Based Modules: Platforms like the Nutrition Module, enabling centralized access and reporting.

By combining desktop, mobile, and web technologies, Winxlibrary ensures flexibility and efficiency for both field operations and centralized analysis workflows.

We prioritize usability, reliability, and real-world impact, supporting evidence-based decision-making for organizations working to improve nutrition and health outcomes.
